About Us

A real estate partner from investment to delivery

Willing Investments For Projects Management is a real estate investment firm based in New Cairo, Egypt. We help investors, developers, and property owners turn opportunities into successful, well-managed projects.

Our services cover the full life cycle of a property: real estate investment, project management, marketing and sales, and ongoing property and asset management. We put clear planning, transparent reporting, and accountability at the center of every engagement.

Whether you are evaluating a new opportunity or need an experienced team to deliver one, we bring structure, discipline, and care to every stage.

What We Do

Our Services

Integrated services that cover every stage of a real estate project.

Real Estate Investment

Identifying, evaluating, and structuring real estate opportunities with market research and feasibility analysis.

Project Management

Planning, scheduling, cost control, and coordination of contractors and stakeholders from design to handover.

Asset Management

Strategic oversight of real estate portfolios to maximize income, optimize performance, and grow long-term asset value.

Property Management

Day-to-day operation, maintenance, leasing, and tenant relations that protect and grow the value of your property.

Real Estate Marketing

Brand positioning, digital campaigns, and sales strategies that connect projects with the right buyers and tenants.

Marketing Services

Content, social media, and performance marketing for businesses that want to grow their reach and sales.
