Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Willing Investments For Projects Management ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects personal information when you visit willinginvestments.com, contact us, or interact with our advertisements, including ads on Meta platforms (Facebook and Instagram).

1. Information We Collect

  • Information you provide: your name, email address, phone number, company name, and any details you share when you contact us or submit an inquiry or lead form.
  • Information collected automatically: device and browser type, IP address, pages visited, and similar usage data collected through cookies and similar technologies.
  • Information from advertising partners: when you respond to our ads (for example, a Meta lead form), we receive the information you choose to submit.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide our services.
  • To send you information about our services, where you have agreed to receive it.
  • To measure and improve our website and advertising.
  • To meet legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations.

3. Cookies and Advertising

We may use cookies and tools such as the Meta Pixel to understand how visitors use our website and to measure our advertising. You can control cookies through your browser settings and manage your ad preferences in your Meta account settings.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it only with trusted service providers who help us run our business (such as hosting, email, and advertising platforms), or when required by law.

5. Data Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

7. Your Rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to stop sending you marketing messages, by contacting us using the details below.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page.
